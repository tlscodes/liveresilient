## Why there is no probability anywhere in this file

Advice reaching this project has twice carried a confident percentage with no
source behind it. NLnet publishes how many projects it selected and does not
publish how many applied, so no acceptance rate can be computed from it.

```

314 selected across eight Commons Fund rounds
applications received: not published
```

The only defensible sentence is that the rate is not published.

## OTF Internet Freedom Fund — the second application

```
who may apply          individuals or organisations, for-profit or nonprofit
range                  10,000 to 900,000 USD for up to 24 months
typical                50,000 to 200,000 USD for 6 to 12 months
intake                 rolling; stage one is a short concept note
instrument             a performance-based contract, paid on completed objectives
relevant category      user research on the needs of people under censorship
```

Two things to hold in mind. The previous application to the Security Lab track
was dismissed for not showing user demand or an open-source commitment, so the
concept note has to name both directly rather than let the reader infer them.
And OTF's own funding was interrupted, with litigation over two appropriation
years still unresolved, so nothing in our documents should state a timeline for
their decision.

## SIDN Fonds — decided by the legal form

```
above 10,000 EUR       "alleen door rechtspersonen" — legal persons only
Pioniers track         maximum 10,000 EUR, year-round, decision within 6 weeks,
                       a natural person may apply
larger applications    decision within 3 months
scope                  the project must have its primary impact in the Netherlands
```

This is the one place the company's legal form is decisive. An *eenmanszaak*
is a business without legal personality, so it is not a *rechtspersoon*: with
the current registration only the Pioniers track is open, and only if a genuine
Dutch use of the work can be stated truthfully. Building a Dutch framing that
does not exist in order to fit the form would fail on its own terms. A BV could
file the larger calls, but a newly incorporated one has no annual accounts to
attach, which those calls ask for.

## Sequence

NLnet first: its published criteria do not require users or letters, it pays
individuals directly, and the small grant is the documented entry ticket to
anything larger. An independent security audit is an eligible activity there,
which turns the gap `SECURITY.md` admits into a funded milestone instead of a
weakness.

OTF second, after the NLnet decision or once the first milestone is running,
because rolling intake costs nothing to wait for and a concept note showing
funded work under way answers the exact ground of the earlier dismissal.

SIDN only if a true Dutch use case exists, as a Pioniers application.
